About Good Boy Records

Good Boy Records is run out of a converted mill in Derbyshire by two golden retrievers with strong opinions about seating arrangements and a passion for chasing balls beyond the gate.

Each cassette on the wall is one generated take. Songs usually have several versions; they sit together in the magazine so you can hear how a track changed between attempts, models or styles.

This page documents how the music is made and acts as a general introduction to AI, generative models and diffusion.

Whilst we are two good dogs, we do have to warn you: some songs, and some of the songs linked from this page, contain explicit language or cover subject matter that may offend.

Please don't play this around your children.

Where'd the idea come from

I've always enjoyed covers, especially when someone completely changes the style or language of the original.

Through the magic of YouTube recommendations, I came across AI covers in 2024. They were quite bad, but very entertaining.

I've kept my eyes and ears open as the technology and the processes have developed, and with some confidence I can state that what you see here at Good Boy Records is the cutting edge of what's available at the time of writing this (Sept. 2026).

I can equally, confidently, state that six months from now this will look like a dog playing a recorder, so enjoy it whilst it's good.
