I've always enjoyed covers, especially when someone completely changes the style
or language of the original.

Through the magic of YouTube recommendations, I came across AI covers in 2024.
They were quite bad, but very entertaining.

I've kept my eyes and ears open as the technology and the processes have developed,
and with some confidence I can state that what you see here at Good Boy Records
is the cutting edge of what's available at the time of writing this (Sept. 2026).

I can equally, confidently, state that six months from now this will look like a
dog playing a recorder, so enjoy it whilst it's good.
