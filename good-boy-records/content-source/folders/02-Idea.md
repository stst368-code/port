Always enjoyed covers. Especially mixing up styles or languages.

Through the magic of YouTube recommendations, I came across AI
covers in 2024 and they were quite bad, but very entertaining.

I've since kept my eyes and ears out as the technology and process
has developed and with some confidence I can state that what you
see here at Good Boy Records is the cutting edge of what's available
at the time of writing this (Sept. 2026).

I can equally confidently state that 6 months from now, this will
look like a dog playing a recorder, so enjoy it whilst it's good.
